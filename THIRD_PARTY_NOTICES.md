## StarNet

The event-contract validation pattern, capability-gate helpers, and the
watchable lead-to-worker handoff concept are adapted from
[androoAGI/starnet](https://github.com/androoAGI/starnet), branch
`feat/harness-backend`, commit
`e316231d70610aac7b181c4efd1a10b3fc833cdc`.

Copyright (c) 2026 Andrew Sims. MIT License.

Files consulted:

- `sidecar/loop.js` (the "every observable transition is a validated bus event"
  invariant and single-use tool gating)
- `sidecar/capability/capGate.js` (adapted: `canAgentUse`, `attenuate`,
  `makeCapCtx`; reproduced with the notice in
  `src/main/workflow/capGate.js`)
- `shared/events.js` (adapted: frozen event contract and boundary validation in
  `src/main/workflow/events.js`)
- `frontend/app/world.js` (adapted concept only: a visible handoff packet whose
  animation is driven solely by workflow events; the Feather implementation in
  `src/renderer/lab.js` is new code and new artwork)

## Artwork

All Feather AI 2.0 artwork in `assets/` and `build/` is generated by
`scripts/generate-art.js` for this project. It contains no third-party art,
fonts, or stock imagery. The three scientist sprites are original pixel art
drawn from geometric primitives in that script.
